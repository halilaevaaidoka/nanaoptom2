Welcome to NANA OPTOM

NANA optom is a Kazakhstan wholesale cosmetics. The brand started in Shymkent in 2020. The name "NANA" means affectionate nickname for the founder. This brand believes that everyone can find what they want.

Our Philosophy

The symbol of NANA is harmony. It represents a balance between strength and tenderness. The brand wants women to feel confident. NANA optom focuses on both inner and outer beauty.

Brand Journey

NANA optom began its journey in 2020. The brand was founded in the city of Shymkent. Today, its products are available throughout Kazakhstan.

What We Offer

NANA optom offers a wide range of wholesale cosmetics for face, lips, eyes, and brows. The collection includes different beauty products for everyday makeup. Prices vary depending on the product. Visitors can explore the full collection on the Products page.
